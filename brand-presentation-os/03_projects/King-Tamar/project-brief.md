# King Tamar - Project Brief

Status: Working
Project: King Tamar
Brand: PRIME Invest

## Project role

King Tamar is the first flagship project inside the PRIME Brand Presentation OS.

It should be treated as a premium urban real estate project in Tbilisi, presented through a structured investor decision journey.

## Core positioning

King Tamar is not only a residential tower. It is a premium urban position on a strong Tbilisi axis, built around location, architecture, lifestyle, benchmark logic and guided investor process.

## Known project anchors

- Project name: King Tamar
- Developer: Archi
- Location: 15 Merab Aleksidze, Tbilisi
- Market frame: premium urban residential project
- Strategic benchmark: King David
- Sales frame: investor meeting, project explanation, unit simulation and next step

## Primary audience

Israeli private investors seeking international real estate exposure, clearer process, premium product, guided decision-making and a structured route from interest to unit-level review.

## Core narrative

1. Tbilisi is developing as an urban market
2. Tourism and city movement support demand in central locations
3. Urban development strengthens the role of the right address
4. King Tamar sits on a strong and memorable axis
5. King David works as a benchmark, not as an enemy
6. King Tamar should be evaluated as a product, not only as price per square meter
7. PRIME provides the process layer: explanation, simulation, documents and guided decision

## Presentation objective

Move the client from curiosity to a structured meeting flow:

- understand the city
- trust the people behind the project
- understand the location
- understand the product
- move to simulation
- define next action

## Current caution points

- Full price list is not placed in the main deck at this stage
- Full payment logic remains a commercial advantage and may be handled through the simulator
- Unit-level numbers belong in Excel or simulation stage
- Legal, tax and banking details require dedicated review before client-facing publication
