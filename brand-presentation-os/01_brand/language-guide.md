# PRIME Invest - Language Guide

Status: Working

## Tone formula

Calm authority + analytical clarity + human guidance + premium restraint

## Writing principles

- Write like an investment committee that knows how to speak human
- Use clear Hebrew and controlled business language
- Lead through structure, not pressure
- Build confidence through process and evidence
- Avoid inflated superlatives
- Keep every sentence useful

## Preferred words

- clarity
- process
- evaluation
- framework
- guidance
- simulation
- fit
- source
- benchmark
- thesis
- decision
- structure
- transparency
- premium
- urban value
- investor fit

## Words and patterns to avoid

- guaranteed return
- crazy opportunity
- only today
- must close now
- no risk
- safe deal
- everyone is buying
- hype language
- unsupported certainty

## Slide headline rule

A headline is a decision statement, not a description.

Weak:
`Project location`

Better:
`A location that turns visibility into demand`

## CTA language

Preferred CTA style:

- Move to a structured fit meeting
- Review the project through numbers and documents
- Open a unit-level simulation
- Move from story to decision framework

## King Tamar language principle

King Tamar should be described as a premium urban position in Tbilisi, not only as a residential tower.
