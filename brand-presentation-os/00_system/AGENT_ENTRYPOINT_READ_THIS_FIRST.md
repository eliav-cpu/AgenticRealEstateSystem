## Non-negotiable rules

1. Do not create a full deck before one master proof slide is approved.
2. First proof slide should be: King David Benchmark Board.
3. King David is a benchmark, not an enemy.
4. King Tamar is the active opportunity side.
5. No VS language.
6. No generic Canva layout.
7. No decorative circles.
8. Every circle must be a Trust Ring, Circular Seal, Laurel KPI, Step Marker, Decision Marker, Benchmark Ring or functional node.
9. A card is not a rectangle. It must feel like a premium system component.
10. A title is not a label. It is a decision command.
11. Premium must not become weak or overly polite.
12. Impact must come from hierarchy, contrast, typography, cards and focal point, not decoration.
13. Use Hebrew RTL by default.
14. Use Heebo as primary typography.
15. Preserve the 70/20/10 color ratio.
