## Global visual diagnosis

The references show that PRIME / King Tamar must operate as a structured decision system, not as a decorative real estate deck.

The strongest slides share the same behavior:

- one big decision headline
- one dominant visual or data object
- cards that work as decision modules
- KPI numbers with strong typographic hierarchy
- circular/laurel motifs used as proof devices
- dark slides used as authority gates
- light boards used for proof, comparison and process
- photo-led slides used for product, lifestyle and trust
- bottom insight lines that frame the conclusion
- no raw Excel feeling
- no generic Canva feeling

## Reference family 01 - Macro KPI cards with laurels

### What appears

Light slide with four KPI cards, each card contains a large blue number, concise label and laurel motifs.

### What works

- Large numbers dominate correctly
- Laurels turn numbers into proof, not decoration
- 2x2 card grid is stable and premium
- Small footnote protects credibility

### DNA extraction

Create a formal Macro KPI Board master.

### Rules

- Use maximum 4 KPI cards
- Number must be the hero, not the label
- Laurel is allowed only for proof-level metrics
- Add a small source / review note
- Avoid overloading with explanations

### Use in King Tamar

Georgia macro, tourism, Tbilisi market, PRIME proof metrics, Archi proof metrics.

## Reference family 02 - Hub diagram with golden ring

### What appears

Large circular ring with central KPI, connected to three driver cards.

### What works

- The ring becomes a functional decision hub
- Driver cards explain why the number matters
- Visual logic is clear: drivers feed the central proof
- Champagne ring adds premium authority without becoming shiny gold

### DNA extraction

Create Decision Hub / Demand Engine master.

### Rules

- Center must contain one dominant KPI or decision point
- Connected cards should explain causes, not repeat the number
- Ring must be a Decision Ring, not background decoration
- Use no more than 3-4 connected cards

### Use in King Tamar

Tourism demand, rental demand, location demand, investor demand, payment flexibility logic.

## Reference family 04 - Transaction process cards

Light board with four cards explaining legal, banking, registration and payment flow.

Create Legal / Financial Process Board master.

## Reference family 05 - Payment timeline lines

### What appears

Three horizontal payment tracks with circular step nodes and percentages.

### What works

- Payment becomes visual and easy to compare
- Circular nodes function as milestones
- Different colors separate track types
- Excellent for A / B / C payment routes

### DNA extraction

Create Payment Track Timeline master.

### Rules

- Each payment route should be one clean line
- Use circular nodes for payment milestones
- Avoid dense legal explanation on the same slide
- Add note that finish / fit-out or separate components may be treated separately when relevant
- Use track labels: A / B / C or Smart / Compass / Infinity only when aligned with actual commercial logic

### Use in King Tamar

3 active payment routes, shell/fit-out clarification, simulator transition.

## New mandatory visual rules

1. Every data slide must decide whether it is a KPI board, decision hub, comparison board or proof board.
2. Every map slide must translate geography into investor logic.
3. Every process slide must reduce uncertainty, not decorate the flow.
4. Every amenity slide must frame facilities as lifestyle infrastructure.
5. Every benchmark slide must highlight King Tamar as active opportunity without attacking King David.
6. Every dark slide must function as authority, transition, proof or CTA.
7. Every laurel/ring/circle must function as proof, decision or trust.
8. Every table must be upgraded into a board.
9. Every large metric must carry a source/review note.
10. Every leadership slide must create trust through role clarity, not biography length.
