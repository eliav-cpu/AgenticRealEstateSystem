## CTA lines

### Soft CTA

- The next step is to move from the general story to a structured fit review.
- From here, the right move is to review the project through numbers, documents and investor fit.
- A short meeting can turn the overview into a clearer decision framework.

### Simulation CTA

- The next stage is a unit-level simulation.
- The story gives context. The simulation tests fit.
- We can now move from project logic to a personal scenario.

### Document review CTA

- The next step is to open the source materials and review the assumptions clearly.
- Before making a decision, the investor should see the documents, numbers and process path.
- The review should move from narrative to source-based evaluation.

## Risk-aware language

Use:

- according to project materials
- based on current available information
- subject to document review
- worth examining
- supports the investment thesis
- may strengthen the project logic
- should be reviewed in a personal simulation

Avoid:

- guaranteed
- certain
- no risk
- safe money
- full peace of mind
- cannot fail
- absolute demand
- guaranteed return
