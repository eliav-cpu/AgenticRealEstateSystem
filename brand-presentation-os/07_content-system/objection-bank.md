# PRIME Objection Bank

Status: Working
Owner: PRIME Invest
Use case: Sales calls, meetings, WhatsApp, follow-ups and presentation speaker notes

## Principle

An objection is not resistance.

It is usually a request for more clarity, more trust or more control.

PRIME should answer objections with:

- respect
- structure
- calm authority
- one clear question
- practical next step

## Response formula

1. Acknowledge
2. Reframe
3. Clarify
4. Offer a structured next step

## Objection 1: I do not know you

### Client says
I do not know you. Why should I trust this?

### Response
That is a fair question. International real estate requires trust before anything else. That is why we do not ask you to decide from a phone call. The right process is to review the project, the people behind it, the documents, the numbers and the fit in a structured way.

### Control question
Would it make sense to start with a short review of who stands behind the project and how the process works?

## Objection 2: I do not invest abroad

### Client says
I do not invest abroad. It feels far and complicated.

### Response
That is exactly why the process matters. The issue is not only the country. It is whether the investor has a clear framework: project, documents, numbers, legal path, payment path and local guidance.

### Control question
Is the concern mainly about the country, the legal process or managing the asset from Israel?

## Objection 3: I need to think about it

### Client says
I need to think about it.

### Response
Of course. A serious decision should not be rushed. The question is what information you need in order to think clearly. Usually, the next useful step is not pressure, but a structured simulation and document review.

### Control question
What would help you think about it properly: numbers, documents or understanding the location better?

## Objection 4: Send me details on WhatsApp

### Client says
Send me details on WhatsApp.

### Response
I can send you a short overview, but the project is easier to understand when it is framed properly. A few messages can show the surface. A structured review can show the logic, the numbers and the fit.

### Control question
Would you prefer I send a short overview first and then we schedule a focused review?

## Objection 5: It sounds expensive

### Client says
It sounds expensive.

### Response
Price should always be examined in context. In premium urban real estate, the question is not only price per square meter. It is location, product level, benchmark, payment path, investor fit and future usability.

### Control question
Would you like to review it through the full cost structure rather than only the headline price?

## Objection 6: I am worried about risk

### Client says
I am worried about risk.

### Response
That is the right instinct. We do not remove risk from the conversation. We organize it. The correct review is to separate project risk, market risk, currency risk, legal risk, payment risk and personal fit.

### Control question
Which risk feels most important for you to understand first?

## Objection 7: I saw many real estate ads like this

### Client says
I see many ads. They all sound the same.

### Response
That is exactly why we focus on process rather than slogans. The project needs to be reviewed through source materials, location logic, benchmark, payment structure, and a personal simulation.

### Control question
Would you like to compare this through a decision framework instead of a marketing message?

## Objection 8: I need my spouse / partner

### Client says
I need to speak with my spouse or partner.

### Response
That makes sense. Investment decisions are better when all decision-makers understand the same picture. The most efficient step is usually to schedule one joint review so everyone sees the same documents and assumptions.

### Control question
Should we schedule a joint review instead of sending pieces of information separately?

## Objection 9: What is the return?

### Client says
What is the return?

### Response
The return question should be answered through a simulation, not a slogan. We need to look at unit, price, payment path, expected use, expenses, scenario assumptions and holding period.

### Control question
Would you like us to build a conservative, base and optimistic scenario in the meeting?

## Objection 10: Why King Tamar?

### Client says
Why this project specifically?

### Response
The project should be examined through several layers: address, product, developer, benchmark, payment path and investor fit. King Tamar is interesting because it combines a premium urban position with a product story and a structured review path.

### Control question
Would you like to start with the location logic or with the product itself?

## Rule for all objections

Do not fight the client.
Do not over-defend the project.
Do not promise certainty.

Return the conversation to:

- source
- process
- fit
- simulation
- next step
