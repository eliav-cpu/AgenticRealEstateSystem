# PRIME Content Pillars

Status: Working
Owner: PRIME Invest
Purpose: Define reusable content pillars beyond presentations

## Principle

PRIME content should not be random.

Every article, WhatsApp, email, post, landing page and video should strengthen one of the core pillars.

## Pillar 1 - Decision clarity

Core idea:

Investors do not need more noise. They need a structured way to decide.

Use for:

- homepage copy
- thought leadership
- meeting CTA
- educational posts
- investor guides

Message examples:

- A serious investment begins with clarity, not pressure.
- The value is not only in finding a project. It is in understanding how to evaluate it.
- PRIME helps turn interest into a structured decision path.

## Pillar 2 - Trust and people

Core idea:

International real estate requires people, process and proof.

Use for:

- founder pages
- leadership slides
- sales scripts
- trust sections
- About pages

Message examples:

- Trust is built through people, process and proof.
- The investor receives a local opportunity through a familiar guidance layer.
- The process matters because the investor needs to know who stands behind each stage.

## Pillar 3 - Source-based evaluation

Core idea:

Every project should be examined through documents, data, assumptions and context.

Use for:

- due diligence content
- investor education
- project pages
- FAQ
- objection handling

Message examples:

- A project should be examined through sources, not slogans.
- Numbers become useful only when they are placed in the right context.
- The right review separates project facts, assumptions and open questions.

## Pillar 4 - Premium urban logic

Core idea:

In real estate, the address is not a detail. It is part of the asset logic.

Use for:

- King Tamar
- location slides
- urban investment articles
- project positioning

Message examples:

- A strong location is understandable, visible and useful.
- The right address turns a building into a position.
- Urban premium real estate should be evaluated through location, product and use.

## Pillar 5 - Process wrapper

Core idea:

The PRIME advantage is the path from first interest to structured next step.

Use for:

- sales scripts
- landing pages
- post-meeting emails
- onboarding flows
- internal playbooks

Message examples:

- The deck creates context. The simulation tests fit.
- The process moves from project review to numbers, documents and next step.
- A clear process reduces friction and helps the investor ask better questions.

## Pillar 6 - Simulation and fit

Core idea:

The question is not only whether a project is good. The question is whether it fits the investor.

Use for:

- meeting CTA
- Excel transition
- financial review
- closing flows

Message examples:

- The next stage is a unit-level simulation.
- A personal scenario is more useful than a generic headline number.
- Fit is built through capital, time horizon, risk profile and payment path.

## Pillar 7 - Risk framing

Core idea:

PRIME does not hide risks and does not dramatize them. PRIME organizes them.

Use for:

- FAQ
- due diligence lite
- objection handling
- investment guides

Message examples:

- A good process does not remove risk from the conversation. It organizes it.
- The investor should understand project risk, market risk, payment risk and personal fit.
- Risk-aware language builds more trust than exaggerated confidence.

## Content rule

Every new content piece should declare which pillar it supports.

If it does not support a pillar, it should not be created.
