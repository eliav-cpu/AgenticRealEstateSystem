# PRIME Brand Voice Linter

Status: Working
Owner: PRIME Invest
Use case: Review marketing, sales and presentation copy before client-facing use

## Purpose

This document is a language control layer for PRIME Invest.

Its job is to prevent copy from becoming:

- too aggressive
- too generic
- too real-estate-sales oriented
- too promising
- too vague
- too heavy
- visually unusable for slides

## PRIME voice formula

Calm authority + analytical clarity + human guidance + premium restraint

## Core test

Before approving any copy, ask:

1. Does it sound like PRIME?
2. Does it build trust before pressure?
3. Does it explain value without overpromising?
4. Does it help the investor understand the decision?
5. Can it sit inside a premium slide without feeling crowded?

## Approved tone

Use language that is:

- precise
- calm
- clear
- investor-oriented
- elegant
- grounded
- human
- structured
- premium

## Avoided tone

Avoid language that is:

- loud
- cheap
- pushy
- absolute
- generic
- overexcited
- manipulative
- unclear
- inflated

## Words to avoid

Do not use in client-facing copy unless legally reviewed and factually supported:

- guaranteed
- no risk
- zero risk
- risk free
- safe money
- certain return
- guaranteed return
- full peace of mind
- immune
- bulletproof
- cannot fail
- everyone is buying
- must close now
- only today
- crazy opportunity
- insane deal
- cash machine

## Preferred replacements

Use controlled alternatives:

- potential
- thesis
- benchmark
- source-based
- should be reviewed
- structured evaluation
- investor fit
- project materials indicate
- according to the available materials
- subject to review
- meaningful layer of confidence
- reduces friction
- supports a clearer process
- worth examining
- decision framework
- simulation
- scenario

## Sentence review checklist

For every sentence, score 1 to 5:

| Criterion | Question | Score |
|---|---|---|
| Clarity | Is the sentence immediately understandable? | 1-5 |
| Trust | Does it increase confidence without exaggeration? | 1-5 |
| Precision | Is it specific enough? | 1-5 |
| Restraint | Is it premium and calm? | 1-5 |
| Usefulness | Does it help the decision? | 1-5 |

Minimum approval score: 20 out of 25

## Slide copy rules

- One idea per slide
- One decision-oriented headline
- Up to 3-5 bullets
- No long paragraphs on screen
- Put explanations in speaker notes
- Use strong nouns, not hype adjectives
- Make the CTA appropriate to the funnel stage

## Funnel CTA rules

### Teaser
Goal: curiosity and meeting

Preferred CTAs:

- Schedule a structured fit call
- Review the project through a clear framework
- Start with a short investor suitability check

### Sales deck
Goal: meeting and simulation

Preferred CTAs:

- Move to a unit-level simulation
- Review the numbers and documents together
- Check investor fit before choosing a unit

### Due diligence lite
Goal: review and decision

Preferred CTAs:

- Open the source documents
- Compare assumptions and scenarios
- Move to legal and financial review

### Closing
Goal: next action

Preferred CTAs:

- Define the unit and payment path
- Prepare the next review step
- Move to reservation subject to review

## Copy classification

Every copy block should be marked as:

- Draft
- Working
- Review
- Approved
- Client Ready
- Archive

## Final approval rule

A text is not approved because it sounds good.

A text is approved when it is:

- aligned with PRIME voice
- factually supported
- legally careful
- useful for the client decision
- visually usable in a premium deck
