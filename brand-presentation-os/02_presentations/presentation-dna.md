# PRIME Presentation DNA

Status: Working

## Core principle

A PRIME presentation must work as a structured decision journey.

It should not feel like:

- a generic brochure
- a random Canva deck
- a dense investment memo
- a loud sales presentation

It should feel like:

- a premium decision system
- a clear investor journey
- a calm boardroom presentation
- a strategic framework with proof layers

## Core narrative flow

1. Big story
2. Market logic
3. Trust and people
4. Location
5. Product
6. Benchmark
7. Numbers
8. Fit
9. Process
10. Next action

## Main slide families

- Cover
- Section divider
- Thesis slide
- Data / KPI slide
- Location slide
- Trust / leadership slide
- Product slide
- Comparison board
- Process slide
- FAQ / risk framing slide
- CTA slide

## Visual modes

### Authority Dark

Navy background, large headline, clean framing, used for covers, dividers, trust, close and high-status moments.

### Strategic Light Board

Light background, cards, grids, KPIs, timelines, comparison boards and decision frameworks.

### Premium Photo Narrative

One strong image with overlay or structured text panel, used for lifestyle, leadership, project story and emotional entry points.

## Slide rules

- One dominant message per slide
- Use structured boards instead of heavy paragraphs
- Prefer cards over long bullet lists
- Use 3 to 5 bullets maximum
- Use numbers only with context
- Make every slide advance the client journey

## King Tamar flow

City -> tourism -> urban development -> location -> King David benchmark -> product -> numbers -> trust -> action
