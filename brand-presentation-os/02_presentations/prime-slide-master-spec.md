# PRIME Slide Master Spec

Status: Working
Owner: PRIME Invest
Use case: Visual source specification for Figma, PPTAgent, ppt-master, Canva and PowerPoint production

## Master principle

A PRIME slide is not a decorative page.

It is a decision unit.

Every slide must answer one question, move one decision forward and feel part of an institutional premium system.

## Required visual modes

### 1. Authority Dark

Use for:

- cover
- chapter divider
- trust moments
- major KPI moments
- closing
- Excel transition

Visual rules:

- PRIME Navy background
- large Heebo headline
- minimal copy
- thin Champagne or Deep Blue accents
- circular trust seal or subtle frame when relevant
- strong whitespace

### 2. Strategic Light Board

Use for:

- market logic
- comparison
- process
- product breakdown
- investor fit
- FAQ
- risk framing

Visual rules:

- White / Cloud / Mist Gray background
- Graphite headline
- card-based structure
- thin borders
- KPI pills
- 3 to 5 primary objects
- no raw Excel tables

### 3. Premium Photo Narrative

Use for:

- project story
- lifestyle
- founder / leadership
- amenities
- location atmosphere

Visual rules:

- one dominant image
- dark overlay when text sits on image
- floating card or side panel
- short emotional copy
- premium restraint

- 70 percent light / white / soft gray
- 20 percent Navy / Graphite
- 10 percent active blue / Champagne

## Typography

Primary font: Heebo

Suggested hierarchy for 16:9 slides:

- H1: 44-58 pt, bold or extra bold
- H2: 30-38 pt, bold
- H3: 20-26 pt, semi bold
- Body: 15-19 pt, regular
- Caption: 10-13 pt, regular
- KPI number: 44-72 pt, bold

Hebrew rules:

- RTL layout
- right aligned by default
- short lines
- avoid dense paragraphs
- use speaker notes for explanation

## Component system

### KPI Card

Use for:

- numbers
- proof
- project data
- market data

Structure:

- large number
- short label
- optional source note

### Trust Card

Use for:

- Archi
- Meni Benish
- Eliav Avshalom
- PRIME
- Benish Group
- process wrapper

Structure:

- icon or portrait
- short title
- 1-2 proof points

### Decision Card

Use for:

- investor fit
- payment path
- process stage
- FAQ
- risk framing

Structure:

- title
- 2-3 short bullets
- subtle number or icon

### Comparison Board

Use for:

- King Tamar vs King David
- product vs market
- location options
- payment tracks

Structure:

- 2-3 columns
- one highlighted column
- bottom insight note
- no spreadsheet look

### Process Line

Use for:

- investor journey
- meeting flow
- purchase process
- source-to-slide workflow

Structure:

- 4-6 steps
- circular step badges
- short labels
- one active step highlight

## Quality gate

Before a full deck is built, create one master proof slide.

A master proof slide must show:

- correct typography
- correct colors
- card system
- RTL behavior
- spacing discipline
- trust motif
- footer system
- clear CTA or decision message

Do not build the full deck before one master slide reaches approval.
