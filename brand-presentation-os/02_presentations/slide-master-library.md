# PRIME Slide Master Library

Status: Working
Owner: PRIME Invest
Purpose: Fixed slide master library for PRIME, King Tamar and future projects

## Principle

The system should not create a new layout every time.

It should reuse approved slide masters and adapt them to the project.

## Master 01 - Cover

Purpose:

- establish premium presence
- introduce project or brand
- create immediate trust and visual authority

Mode:

- Authority Dark or Premium Photo Narrative

Required elements:

- project title
- short positioning line
- PRIME logo
- optional project image
- minimal text

## Master 02 - Thesis

Purpose:

- explain the central investment or brand thesis
- set the logic of the presentation

Mode:

- Strategic Light Board

Required elements:

- large decision headline
- 3-5 thesis cards
- short bottom insight

## Master 03 - Market Logic

Purpose:

- explain why the market context matters
- avoid generic macro slides

Mode:

- Strategic Light Board

Required elements:

- 3-4 market drivers
- source note
- insight line

## Master 04 - Location Board

Purpose:

- translate location into investor logic

Mode:

- Strategic Light Board / Premium Photo Narrative

Required elements:

- map / image / location axis
- 3-5 location proof points
- one address logic message

## Master 05 - Benchmark Comparison

Purpose:

- compare without attacking
- frame product positioning

Mode:

- Strategic Light Board

Required elements:

- 2-3 comparison columns
- one highlighted active opportunity
- bottom insight card
- no raw table look

## Master 06 - KPI Grid

Purpose:

- present core facts quickly

Mode:

- Strategic Light Board

Required elements:

- 4-6 KPI cards
- large numbers
- source labels
- one framing line

## Master 07 - Trust Founder Split

Purpose:

- create human authority

Mode:

- Premium Photo Narrative

Required elements:

- large portrait or leadership image
- short biography block
- 3 trust points
- no long biography wall

## Master 08 - Trust Company Grid

Purpose:

- show institutional credibility

Mode:

Required elements:

- Archi

- Meni Benish / Eliav Avshalom
- process wrapper
- proof cards

## Master 09 - Product Showcase

Purpose:

- explain what the product is

Mode:

- Premium Photo Narrative / Strategic Light Board

Required elements:

- hero rendering or image
- product description
- 3-5 product layers

## Master 10 - Amenities Board

Purpose:

- show lifestyle and usability without catalog overload

Mode:

- Strategic Light Board

Required elements:

- 4 category cards
- icons or minimal images
- short labels

## Master 11 - Payment Tracks

Purpose:

- explain payment logic without exposing too much commercial detail

Mode:

- Strategic Light Board

Required elements:

- 2-4 tracks
- staged logic
- review note
- CTA to simulation

## Master 12 - Process Timeline

Purpose:

- reduce friction and uncertainty

Mode:

- Strategic Light Board

Required elements:

- 4-6 steps
- circular step badges
- active step highlight

## Master 13 - FAQ / Risk Board

Purpose:

- answer difficult questions calmly

Mode:

- Strategic Light Board

Required elements:

- 4-6 question cards
- careful wording
- source/review note when needed

## Master 14 - Excel Transition

Purpose:

- move from story to simulation

Mode:

- Authority Dark

Required elements:

- short headline
- 3 simulation inputs
- one CTA
- no spreadsheet screenshot unless polished

## Master 15 - CTA / Closing

Purpose:

- define the next step clearly

Mode:

- Authority Dark or Strategic Light Board

Required elements:

- one next action
- no pressure language
- contact / meeting path

## Reuse rule

Every new deck should start from this library.

If a new slide type is needed, create it as a new master and add it here.
