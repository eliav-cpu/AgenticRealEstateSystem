## Visual Positioning
Private Banking meets Premium Urban Real Estate.

The visual system must feel:
- institutional
- premium
- calm
- precise
- urban
- investor-grade
- not generic real-estate advertising

## Core Modes
1. Authority Dark
2. Strategic Light Board
3. Premium Photo Narrative

## Color System
- Institutional Navy `#0A1E3F`
- EXEL Blue `#0066FF`
- Silver Gray `#D8DADD`
- Pearl Gray `#F3F4F5`
- Pure White `#FFFFFF`
- Royal Champagne `#B7A47D`
- Supporting Graphite `#394452`

## Color Ratio
- 70% white / light grey
- 20% navy / graphite
- 10% CTA blue / champagne

Champagne is a restrained premium accent only. Never use it as loud gold.
EXEL Blue is for isolated emphasis, buttons and active states only.

## Typography
- Heebo-style Hebrew typography.
- Full RTL.
- Right alignment.
- Big decision-oriented titles.
- Short subtitles.
- Minimal body copy.
- Large enough for meeting-room readability.

## Zero Typography Error Rule
One typo equals failed output.

Failed output includes:
- broken Hebrew
- reversed Hebrew
- blurred text
- fake generated letters
- smeared typography
- unreadable labels
- inconsistent hierarchy
- left-aligned Hebrew

If image generation may damage Hebrew text, generate the visual/background separately and add final Hebrew typography in a controlled design layer.

## Dual Colorway Rule
Every strategic slide must support two structurally identical variants:

### Light
White / Pearl Gray / Mist background, navy typography, white cards, subtle champagne and controlled blue accents.

### Dark
Institutional Navy / Graphite background, white typography, subtle champagne accent, calm private-banking atmosphere.

Do not redesign the slide. Duplicate the same structure and recolor.
