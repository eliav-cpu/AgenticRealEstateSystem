# PRIME / King Tamar Visual DNA

## Classification
Visual Source of Truth | PRIME / King Tamar | RTL Hebrew

## Visual Positioning
Private Banking meets Premium Urban Real Estate.

The visual system must feel:
- institutional
- premium
- calm
- precise
- urban
- investor-grade
- not generic real-estate advertising

## Core Modes
1. Authority Dark
2. Strategic Light Board
3. Premium Photo Narrative

## Color System
- PRIME Navy: `#0F1E3A`
- Deep Blue: `#2F63C8`
- CTA Blue: `#4D8DF7`
- White: `#FFFFFF`
- Cloud: `#FAFAFB`
- Mist Gray: `#F3F4F6`
- Graphite: `#111827`
- Steel Gray: `#6B7280`
- Champagne Sand: `#D6C7A1`

## Color Ratio
- 70% white / light grey
- 20% navy / graphite
- 10% CTA blue / champagne

Champagne is a restrained premium accent only. Never use it as loud gold.
CTA Blue is for isolated emphasis, buttons and active states only.

## Typography
- Heebo-style Hebrew typography.
- Full RTL.
- Right alignment.
- Big decision-oriented titles.
- Short subtitles.
- Minimal body copy.
- Large enough for meeting-room readability.

## Zero Typography Error Rule
One typo equals failed output.

Failed output includes:
- broken Hebrew
- reversed Hebrew
- blurred text
- fake generated letters
- smeared typography
- unreadable labels
- inconsistent hierarchy
- left-aligned Hebrew

If image generation may damage Hebrew text, generate the visual/background separately and add final Hebrew typography in a controlled design layer.

## Card System
Cards are the core thinking unit.

Required card families:
- KPI Cards
- Trust Cards
- Track Cards
- Process Cards
- FAQ Cards
- Risk Cards
- Comparison Cards
- Decision Cards

Cards must feel like branded decision units, not generic rectangles.

## Circular Trust Language
Circularity is not decoration. It is a trust layer.

Use:
- laurel KPI badges
- trust rings
- circular seals
- step badges
- achievement markers
- journey points

## Slide Masters
Required master templates:
- Cover
- Thesis
- KPI Grid
- Prime of Prime Location Board
- King David Comparison Board
- Founder / Leadership Split Screen
- Trust Triangle
- Payment Tracks
- Excel Transition / Decision Gate
- FAQ
- CTA

## Dual Colorway Rule
Every strategic slide must support two structurally identical variants:

### Light
White / Cloud / Mist background, navy typography, white cards, subtle champagne and controlled blue accents.

### Dark
PRIME Navy / Graphite background, white typography, subtle champagne accent, calm private-banking atmosphere.

Do not redesign the slide. Duplicate the same structure and recolor.

## Avoid
- generic Canva look
- shiny gold
- loud blue
- clutter
- over-designed luxury
- tiny text
- raw Excel tables on slides
- fake AI stock look
- altered faces of real people

## Image Prompt Minimum Requirements
Every image/design prompt should include:
- 16:9 investor slide
- PRIME / King Tamar DNA
- RTL Hebrew
- Heebo-style typography
- Light and Dark variants if strategic
- official PRIME colors
- clean card hierarchy
- zero typography errors
- no invented numbers
- no guaranteed-return language
- do not alter real people's faces
