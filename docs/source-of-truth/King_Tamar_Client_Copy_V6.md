# King Tamar Client Copy V6 — Source of Truth

## Classification
Client Deck Copy | Source of Truth | RTL Hebrew | PRIME / King Tamar

## Purpose
This document controls only what appears on client-facing King Tamar slides.

It defines:
- headlines
- subtitles
- card copy
- optional screen message lines
- IMAGE_EDIT_PROMPT direction
- two headline variations per slide
- recommended client-facing title

## Hard Separation Rule
This file is for screen copy only.
Do not add salesperson scripts, objections, internal explanations, psychological rationale, legal notes or team training to client-facing slides.

## Writing Rules
- Hebrew-first.
- RTL and right aligned.
- Simple, modern, premium, classic and clear for Israeli investors.
- Clear within 3 seconds.
- Investor-facing, value/proof/action oriented.
- Short headline, short subtitle, 3–4 concise cards.
- Message line only if it adds real value on the screen.
- No long explanations.
- No heavy consultant language.

Avoid:
- פוזיציה
- סקייל
- סטייל
- מיקרו־לוקיישן
- מערכת החלטה
- article-style headlines
- research-style headlines
- negative headline openings such as לא עוד / לא רק

## Commercial / Data Rule
Every numeric, financial, regulatory or commercial claim must be source-verified before client use.
If no source exists, write: `דורש אימות`.
Never invent data.
Never use guaranteed-return language.

## Headline Variation Rule
For every uploaded or rewritten slide, first provide exactly two headline options:

1. סיפורי־יוקרתי — premium, story-led, elegant, emotional but clear.
2. ברור־מכירתי — direct, simple, value-led, easy to understand in 3 seconds.

Then choose one recommended headline for the client-facing slide.

## IMAGE_EDIT_PROMPT Rules
Every image/design prompt must include:
- 16:9 investor slide
- RTL Hebrew
- Heebo-style typography
- full right alignment
- PRIME colors only
- light and dark matched variants where strategic
- no smeared text
- no fake Hebrew
- no Canva generic style
- no loud blue
- Champagne only as subtle accent
- do not alter real faces

## Fast Screen-Copy Rule
When in doubt, shorten. The guide explains; the slide sells the idea and advances the decision.
