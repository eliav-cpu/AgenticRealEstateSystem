# PRIME / King Tamar Slide 100 Scorecard

## Purpose
A simple scoring system for every King Tamar slide, banner, visual or investor-facing asset.

## Score Bands

| Score | Meaning | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 95–100 | Investor-ready PRIME standard | Deliver / use as approved reference |
| 85–94 | Strong, needs polish | Minor correction before delivery |
| 70–84 | Working draft | Structural refinement required |
| 50–69 | Weak | Rebuild copy or hierarchy |
| 0–49 | Failed | Do not use |

## Weighted Criteria

### 1. Source Discipline — 15 points
- 15: Based on correct source layer, no mixing, all data sourced or marked.
- 10: Mostly sourced, minor unclear claim.
- 5: Several source gaps.
- 0: Invented or unsupported claims.

### 2. Client Screen Clarity — 15 points
- 15: Understood within 3 seconds.
- 10: Clear but slightly heavy.
- 5: Requires explanation.
- 0: Confusing.

### 3. Headline Strength — 10 points
- 10: Simple, premium, clear, decision-oriented.
- 7: Good but could be sharper.
- 4: Descriptive or too clever.
- 0: Wrong / confusing / negative framing.

### 4. Copy Structure — 10 points
- 10: Short subtitle, 3–4 strong cards, no clutter.
- 7: Slightly heavy.
- 4: Too much text.
- 0: Screen reads like a document.

### 5. Investor Decision Logic — 15 points
- 15: Moves client toward trust, product, Excel, unit choice or reservation.
- 10: Has value but weak next step.
- 5: Nice but not decision-oriented.
- 0: Decorative only.

### 6. PRIME Visual DNA — 15 points
- 15: Institutional premium, correct colors, cards, spacing, hierarchy.
- 10: Mostly on-brand.
- 5: Generic but usable.
- 0: Canva-like / off-brand.

### 7. RTL / Typography — 10 points
- 10: Perfect Hebrew RTL, Heebo-style, sharp and readable.
- 7: Minor visual issue.
- 4: Small text or hierarchy issue.
- 0: Any typo, smeared Hebrew or broken RTL.

### 8. Commercial Safety — 10 points
- 10: No promises, no risky language, correct qualifiers.
- 7: Minor wording risk.
- 4: Needs compliance rewrite.
- 0: Contains guarantees or unsafe claims.

## Automatic Fail Conditions
Any of these caps the score at 49:
- Hebrew typo on final visual
- broken RTL
- smeared or fake Hebrew text
- guaranteed-return language
- invented number
- altered real person’s face
- Client Copy and Sales Playbook mixed on screen

A 95+ slide is not just beautiful.
It is clear, sourced, sharp, RTL-correct, commercially safe and useful in a live investor meeting.
