## Required Slide Standard
Every important slide must be planned or delivered in two matched versions:

1. PRIME Light / Grey
- white / cloud / mist grey base
- navy text
- controlled PRIME blue accent
- institutional, clean, website-like

2. PRIME Dark / Navy / Black / Champagne
- navy / black base
- white text
- restrained champagne / muted gold accent
- premium, calm, authoritative

Critical rule: Same structure, same message, same hierarchy. Only the colorway changes.

## Zero Typography Rule
A slide or image with one typography mistake is failed output.

Failed output includes:
- Hebrew typo
- broken RTL
- reversed words
- blurry text
- fake generated letters
- distorted numerals
- unreadable labels
- inconsistent hierarchy

If image generation may damage Hebrew text, generate the background/image separately and add Hebrew typography in the slide/PPT layer.
